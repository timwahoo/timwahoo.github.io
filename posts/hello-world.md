Welcome. This is the first post — a small place to put things that don't fit in a paper, a talk, or a caption.

I've been keeping notes in scattered files for years: half-finished derivations, debugging logs, opinions about reconstruction that never made it into a manuscript. Most of it is only useful to me. Some of it might be useful to someone else. This is where the second category goes.

## What to expect

- **Field notes from the lab** — what worked, what didn't, and the assumptions that quietly broke.
- **Code and tooling** — small things I build, and the reasons they exist.
- **The occasional detour** — coffee, swimming, synths, and whatever else keeps the work sustainable.

Not every post will be about MRI. Some will. The tags on each entry should make the difference obvious.

## How this is built

The site is still plain HTML, CSS and ES modules. Posts are Markdown files, metadata lives in one data file, and a small renderer turns the two into the page you're reading. No build step, no framework, no database.

> The best writing setup is the one you actually use. This one is deliberately boring.

If something here is wrong, unclear, or worth arguing about, the contact links are in the footer. I'd rather hear it than not.
